Before you play Synchronicity

Synchronicity uses online services to maintain your play session, measure playtime, and protect the backend against abuse.

When you continue, the game may process:

  • An anonymous player identifier and an optional player name
  • Session start/end times, activity status, and play duration
  • Approximate country and city inferred from your IP address
  • IP-based information used for geolocation and network abuse prevention

The backend uses Supabase and IPinfo. Read the full Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) for details, retention, and your rights.

Continuing starts online session tracking. The button acknowledges that you've been shown this notice; it is not a substitute for any consent required by law.
