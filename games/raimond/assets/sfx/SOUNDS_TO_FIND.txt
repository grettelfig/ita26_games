SOUNDS TO FIND
==============
ALL FOUND (v56): your recordings are built into the game (js/p2_sfxdata.js). Footsteps were cut into single
steps (6 each for wood, tile and carpet), the tap and the clock were made to loop. Anything dropped in this folder
under the same names still takes over.

Drop each file in this folder with exactly the name below (.ogg, .mp3 or .wav). The game picks it up on its own
and stops using its generated stand-in. For sounds that repeat a lot (footsteps, creaks, knocks) you can add
variants: name_2, name_3, name_4. The game picks one at random each time, so it doesn't sound like a loop.

Free sources: freesound.org (filter: Creative Commons 0), pixabay.com/sound-effects, the Sonniss GDC bundles.
If a sound is CC-BY, note the author so it can go in the credits.

1. footstep_wood   (+ footstep_wood_2, _3, _4)
   Her own steps on wooden floors: halls, stairs, bedrooms.
   Search: "footstep wood floor single", "footsteps socks wooden floor", "barefoot wooden floor step"
   Want: ONE step per file, about 0.2-0.5 s, soft (socks or bare feet, indoors), no shoes clacking, no reverb tail.
   (A recording of someone walking is fine too: send it and I'll cut it into single steps.)

2. footstep_tile   (+ footstep_tile_2, _3, _4)
   Her steps on the kitchen, bathroom and downstairs hall tiles.
   Search: "footstep tile barefoot", "footsteps socks tile floor", "bathroom floor footstep"
   Want: same as above, one step per file, a little harder and brighter than the wood.

3. door_creak   (+ door_creak_2, _3)
   An old interior wooden door being opened slowly. Plays for every door that opens.
   Search: "door creak slow", "old wooden door creak open", "creaky door opening"
   Want: 1-2.5 s, the creak only (no latch click, no slam at the end), no background noise.
   (Doors closing already have their sound.)

4. tap_running
   The kitchen tap that's running on day 2 ("You didn't turn it on"), heard from across the house.
   Search: "kitchen faucet running loop", "tap water running sink", "faucet running steel sink"
   Want: a steady stream into a sink, 5-20 s, ideally made to loop, no hands washing, no tap being turned.

5. pipes   (+ pipes_2, _3)
   Knocking in the walls now and then, more on day 2.
   Search: "pipe knocking", "radiator banging", "water hammer pipes", "heating pipes knock"
   Want: 1-4 s of a few dull metallic knocks, no hissing or running water.

6. clock_tick
   A ticking clock for the downstairs hall. The house has no clock there yet: I'll put one in when the sound comes in.
   Search: "wall clock ticking loop", "grandfather clock tick tock", "pendulum clock ticking"
   Want: a slow tick-tock (about one per second), 4-10 s and loopable, no chimes.

Until a file is here, the game plays its generated stand-in for 1-5 (the clock waits for its file).
